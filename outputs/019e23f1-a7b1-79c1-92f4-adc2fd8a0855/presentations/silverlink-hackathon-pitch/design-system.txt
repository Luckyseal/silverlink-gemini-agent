# Design System

Canvas: 16:9, 1280 x 720.

Visual language:
- Calm product keynote, not a healthcare dashboard.
- One recurring ambient orb to signal quiet presence.
- Big human thesis first, then concrete system proof.
- Large Japanese UI fragments for demo credibility.
- Architecture is lane-based and explicit: signals, Gemini, agents, actions, safety.

Color:
- Ink #10141C
- Ink 2 #141B26
- Deep panel #1B2431
- Paper #F4EFE5
- Mint #7CD6C4
- Amber #F2C14E
- Coral #F08A77
- Blue #8CA7FF
- White #FFFFFF

Type:
- Hero claims: 44-58 px, high contrast.
- Body: 18-22 px, short lines.
- Mono labels: 11-13 px for system tags and slide numbers.
- Japanese copy: large and sparse, never dashboard-like.

Layout rhythm:
- 1: title / final positioning.
- 2: problem as silence.
- 3: insight, technology adapts to people.
- 4: product interface verbs.
- 5: 90-second demo storyboard.
- 6: architecture.
- 7: why Gemini.
- 8: safety boundaries.
- 9: Japan first, global next.
- 10: closing line.
