# Claim Spine

1. SilverLink is Ambient Family Intelligence for aging societies.
2. The most dangerous signal is silence.
3. Older adults do not need another app to learn; technology should adapt to them.
4. One ambient interface can see, listen, remember, gently act, and hand off to humans.
5. The demo shows silence becoming a gentle conversation, then a human-readable handoff.
6. Gemini provides the coherent multimodal reasoning stack behind the agent.
7. The agent architecture separates perception, memory, triage, communication, action, and safety.
8. Safety is a product principle: no diagnosis, no dosage change, human escalation, privacy-first memory.
9. Japan is the first wedge; the global opportunity is remote family care as an ambient relationship layer.
10. Technology should not replace relationships. It should quietly protect them.

Tone guardrails:
- Do not say AI elderly-care app, senior health app, medication reminder, monitoring dashboard, or care SaaS.
- Say Ambient Family Intelligence, quiet presence, silent signal detection, gentle intelligence, and human handoff.
- Keep the demo emotionally clear and technically bounded.
