# Contact Sheet Plan

Goal: At thumbnail size, judges should see a winner-level product story: human problem, ambient interface, concrete demo, Gemini architecture, safety, and global vision.

Expected thumbnail rhythm:
1. Title and positioning with one calm orb.
2. Dark silence slide with four weak daily signals.
3. Split insight: "another app" versus "ambient layer".
4. Five verb interface: see, listen, remember, gently act, hand off.
5. Two-minute solo demo storyboard with Google Cloud voice visible.
6. Architecture stack from environment to safety.
7. Google Cloud + Gemini capability constellation.
8. Safety boundary matrix.
9. Japan/global expansion map.
10. Closing quote and strongest line.

Review gates:
- Does it avoid the visual language of an elderly-care app or admin dashboard?
- Can a judge retell the story from thumbnails alone?
- Is Gemini visible as perception and reasoning, not just a chatbot?
- Is Google Cloud visible enough for the event's eligibility rule?
- Are safety boundaries clear and calm?
- Are speaker notes short enough for shadow reading?
